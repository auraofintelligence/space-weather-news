Build brief

Why Space Weather News exists.

The background to the build: the vision, the honest layering, the code already in hand, the proposed architecture and the roadmap. The v0 spec itself lives in the build plan; this page is the why and the context.

Standalone macro project. Kept separate from the other builds. No Aura, XR or horn-tori interface, and no Sensorium wiring, at this stage.

01The vision, in Luke's frame

An XR/AI space-weather news, monitoring and early-warning hub. Watch the Sun and the near-Earth environment in real time; fuse many signals into one picture; surface what actually matters to power grids, satellites, aviation, communications and people; and, as a flagged research frontier, model whether space-weather and geophysical precursors can give early warning of large (M7+) earthquakes. Delivered as a fast, honest public web experience.

It is one of Luke's long-held pillars: his LinkedIn calls it "XR/AI Earthquake Prediction Modelling · SpaceWeather News". It is a standalone product. Wiring it into the Global Sensorium or the Aura interface is out of scope for now; the macro projects stay separate.

02Surface the gem: the honest layering

Lead with what is real and label the rest truthfully. This part is non-negotiable. Three layers:

Real today · build first

Monitoring and impact alerts

Real-time space-weather monitoring and operational-impact alerting. NOAA SWPC, NASA (DONKI, SDO), USGS and GNSS data are public and live: solar wind speed and density, IMF Bz, Kp and Ap, X-ray flux, flares, CMEs, aurora ovals, proton flux.

The geomagnetic storm to grid, satellite, aviation and HF-comms impact layer is operationally real; NOAA SWPC does exactly this. A fast, honest dashboard and news feed is buildable now, no permission required.

Legitimate research

Signal fusion and ML

Multi-source signal fusion: the 21-point "IFTTT" architecture from the docs, covering solar wind, IMF, TEC and the ionosphere, ULF electromagnetics, radon, InSAR ground deformation, thermal infrared, acoustic and hydrochemical signals, feeding ML pattern recognition.

The sensor-fusion and alerting architecture is sound and reusable whatever the headline claim does.

Contested · flagged

Earthquake early warning

M7+ earthquake early warning from space-weather and geophysical precursors. Built as a clearly labelled experimental module: confidence scores, transparent features, rigorous backtesting against historical catalogues, and prominent "research, not a warning" framing.

Never presented as fact or as a warning. The licence (section 5) requires this.

This layering is itself the lesson from Luke's "surface the gem" rule: don't let a contested headline bury a useful, real-today architecture.

03The real code already exists

space-weather-hub-pseudo-code.md contains working Python to build from, not just prose:

  • InfluxDB write_data and capture_data for time-series ingest;
  • a Kafka producer for streaming;
  • a RandomForestClassifier earthquake model plus correlation analysis.

Treat it as the reference implementation. The build can start simpler (client-side) and grow into this streaming and ML backbone.

04Proposed architecture

The starting point, to decide together. Data flows left to right:

SourcesNOAA SWPC, NASA DONKI and SDO, USGS quakes, GNSS and TEC, and more
IngestPoll and fetch
StreamKafka, or something lighter
StoreInfluxDB or a lightweight time-series database
Fuse + MLSignal fusion, RandomForest and onward
ServeREST and WebSocket
Experiences1. Web dashboard and news (v0)
2. Ops-impact alerts
3. VR/AI "Aura" news (Unity), later feeding the Global Sensorium
  • v0 needs no backend. The public NOAA SWPC JSON products can be fetched in the browser. Ship a static dashboard first (GitHub Pages, .nojekyll), like Luke's other repos.
  • Add the Kafka and InfluxDB streaming plus ML backbone as Phase 2, porting the pseudo-code.
  • The VR/XR and any "Aura" interface are deferred, out of scope for now. Near-term is a clean, fast web UI only.

05First milestone

A live, honest web dashboard, client-side and static-deployable, showing right now:

  • solar wind (speed, density, Bz), a Kp index gauge, X-ray flux (flare class), proton flux;
  • active regions and the latest flares and CMEs (NASA DONKI), and the aurora forecast oval;
  • a news ticker that turns significant events into plain-language headlines, for example "G2 geomagnetic storm underway: high-latitude grids on watch, aurora possible to about 50° latitude";
  • an ops-impact panel: grids, satellites, aviation and HF comms status at a glance.

Design bar: Aura house style, cosmic, classy and alive (the an-open-book design system is the reference). Australian English. Nothing about hazards overstated.

This milestone is what the dashboard runs today.

06Roadmap

  1. v0 · Live dashboard and news (client-side, real NOAA and NASA data).
  2. v1 · Streaming and fusion backbone (Kafka to InfluxDB to signal fusion; port the pseudo-code).
  3. v2 · Research module (ML earthquake backtesting, transparent and flagged; the AUS-IND hackathon framing is in the docs).
  4. v3 · VR/AI "Aura" experience (Unity; horn-tori interface, learning the form first).
  5. Sensorium integration: publish the fused feed as the Global Sensorium's first data domain.

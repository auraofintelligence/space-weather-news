North star: a global VR news studio

Space Weather News is global from the start, at macro and planetary scale. Its destination is a VR news studio: an immersive newsroom and mission control with screens for all the macro data (solar wind, Kp, X-rays and flares, CMEs, proton flux, aurora and other global feeds), each a live screen, broadcast style.

Corpus: space-weather-news-hub-vrai, vr-space-weather-news, modular-unity-development-deployment-plan-for-spaceweather-news-hub.

This is a macro-data-display project, a different style from the place-twin Sensorium. The v0 web dashboard is the data layer that will feed the studio's screens.

Goal of v0

A live, client-side static web dashboard and plain-language news feed off real public space-weather data. It opens by double-clicking index.html and deploys to GitHub Pages unchanged. Honest about hazards: guidance, never official warnings.

Stack

  • Vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no build step, no dependencies beyond two optional Google Fonts.
  • Fetch NOAA SWPC and NASA JSON directly in the browser.
  • Charts via inline SVG or <canvas>; no chart library needed.
  • Reuse the an-open-book design language: deep cosmic ink, violet and gold, Fraunces and Inter, starfield.

Tasks, in order

  1. Scaffold the static site; port the an-open-book tokens, typography and starfield into styles.css. Sticky header, responsive, reduced-motion, accessible.
  2. data.js: one fetch-and-normalise function per endpoint; graceful error and stale handling; a single refreshAll() on a 90-second interval; timestamps ("as of … UTC").
  3. Current conditions header: the three NOAA scales (R radio blackouts, S solar radiation, G geomagnetic) as live badges from noaa-scales.json, colour-coded, with the plain-English meaning.
  4. Gauges and sparklines: Kp (0 to 9 dial), solar-wind speed and density, IMF Bz (highlight southward, negative values), X-ray flux with flare class (A/B/C/M/X). Vanilla SVG or canvas.
  5. News ticker: turn alerts.json into plain-language headlines, for example "⚠️ G2 (Moderate) geomagnetic storm in progress: high-latitude power grids on watch; aurora possible toward mid-latitudes." Most recent first; no off-site links.
  6. Ops-impact panel: grids, satellites, and aviation and HF comms status derived from Kp, flare class and proton flux thresholds. Every row labelled "guidance, not an official warning; see NOAA SWPC."
  7. Aurora and active regions: OVATION probability (a simple hemisphere view or a "visible to about X° latitude tonight" line) and a sunspot region list.
  8. Polish: loading, empty and error states; mobile layout; a footer with data-source credits, the licence and the Luke × Claude signature. .nojekyll. Confirm it runs from file:// and is Pages-ready.

Acceptance

Double-clicking index.html shows real, current space weather that refreshes. The news ticker reads like plain English. Ops-impact carries honest disclaimers. It looks great, throws no console errors, and works on mobile.

Explicitly not in v0

  • No Aura, horn-tori or XR.
  • No earthquake prediction. That is a later, clearly flagged research module, not now.
  • No backend, and no Kafka, InfluxDB or ML yet. That is v1.
  • Nothing presented as an official warning.
  • No coupling to web3-sensorium.

Decisions for Luke

  • Public-facing news tone versus technical ops console, or one view with a toggle? Default: one view, plain language first, details expandable.
  • Include the DEMO_KEY NASA DONKI CME feed in v0, or wait for a real key?
  • Domain and branding for the eventual Pages deploy.

Where v0 landed. The dashboard ships as one view with a News / Ops Console toggle, and includes the DONKI CME feed on the shared DEMO_KEY with a clear "demo data, rate-limited" label. Domain and branding remain open.

Later phases

  • v1: streaming and fusion backbone, porting space-weather-hub-pseudo-code.md (Kafka to InfluxDB to signal fusion).
  • v2: the flagged earthquake research module: backtested, transparent, never a warning.
